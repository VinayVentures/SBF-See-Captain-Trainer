# 🌊 SBF-See Captain Trainer — Quick Guide

**Live app:** https://vinayventures.github.io/SBF-See-Captain-Trainer/

A bilingual (Deutsch / English) study companion for the German **Sportbootführerschein See** exam. Free, no signup required, works offline once installed.

---

## 📲 Install it (2 minutes)

**iPhone / iPad:**
1. Open the link above in **Safari** (not Chrome — must be Safari on iOS)
2. Tap the **Share** button
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**
4. Tap **Add**

**Android:**
1. Open in **Chrome**
2. Tap the **⋮ menu** (top right)
3. Tap **Install app** (or **Add to Home Screen**)

**Desktop (Mac/Windows):**
- Chrome/Edge: click the install icon (⊕) in the address bar
- Or just use it in the browser — everything works either way

Once installed, it works offline. Your progress lives on your device.

## 🎓 The five study modes

### 1. ⚓ Continue learning (main mode)
Tap **Start voyage**. The app picks questions smartly:
- Questions you got wrong recently come up first
- Then new questions you haven't seen
- Then review of things you're getting right (spaced out over time)

For each question:
- **Answer in German** (top pane — that's the exam language)
- **English mirror** below shows the same options translated (labeled as study aid, not official)
- **📝 Add your own notes** — persists per question
- **☆ Bookmark** anything you want to revisit
- **ⓘ Explain** button after answering shows why the answer is correct + a memory aid

### 2. 🛢 The Bilge (weak questions review)
The app tracks questions you get wrong. Tap **Review** to drill only those until you master them.

### 3. ★ Bookmarks
Focused study on just the questions you flagged.

### 4. 📝 Mock exam — the real deal
Simulates the official exam:
- ⏱ **60-minute timer** (auto-submits when time runs out)
- **7 Basis** questions (pass ≥ 5/7)
- **23 See-specific** questions (pass ≥ 18/23)
- **9 navigation** questions (pass ≥ 7/9)
- **No feedback during the exam** — same as the real thing
- Navigate freely between questions, change answers before submitting
- After submitting: full review, per-section scores, PASS/not-yet verdict

**For nav questions:** work them on your physical **D49 chart** (as you'll do in the real exam), type your answer, then self-grade after submitting.

### 5. 🧭 Navigation Academy
All 15 official navigation task templates + 8 D49 chart scenarios. Structured input fields (courses, coordinates, distances, times) plus a scratchpad for your working.

## 🪢 Knoten Academy

All 9 practical-exam knot skills:
- Achtknoten, Kreuzknoten, Palstek, Schotstek, Stopperstek, Webleinstek, Webleinstek auf Slip, Rundtörn mit zwei halben Schlägen, Klampenbelegen

Each knot has bilingual step-by-step instructions, animated visualization, and a **Mastered** checkbox when you can tie it AND explain its use.

> ⚠️ The animated diagrams are schematic — please still practice with real rope and check against a knot book (Ashley Book of Knots is the standard reference).

## 🎮 Gamification (makes it stick)

- **XP** — earn 10 per correct answer, 2 per wrong (yes, wrong still counts for effort)
- **🌬 Wind Streak** — bonus XP for correct-in-a-row: +15 at 5, +40 at 10, +100 at 25, +250 at 50
- **🎯 Daily Missions** — 3 new challenges every day, +25–50 XP each on completion
- **🏆 8 Achievements** to unlock — COLREG Master, Lights & Signals, Navigation Officer, Knot Master, Fair Winds (50 streak), First Voyage (first passed mock), Halfway Home (50% mastered), Chartered (100%)

## 💾 Backing up your progress

- **Export progress** on the Bridge → saves a JSON file to your device. Keep this if you switch devices or clear your browser.
- **Import progress** → restore from that JSON file.

There's no cloud sync yet (planned for v1.3.0 final). For now the JSON file is your backup — email it to yourself occasionally if you're serious about not losing progress.

## 💡 Study tips

1. **Start with a mock exam early** — get a baseline. Don't worry about the score.
2. **Focus on the Bilge** — the app already knows what you're weak on. Use it.
3. **Daily missions keep the streak alive** — even 10 questions a day beats cramming.
4. **Bookmark tricky ones** — questions with subtle wording (`0,5‰` vs `0,8‰`) are worth flagging.
5. **Do the navigation on a real D49 chart** — the digital scratchpad is only a supplement.
6. **Use the memory aids** — the 💡 hints under explanations are designed to be sticky.
7. **Aim for 100% mastery** in your weakest topic before a real mock — the Bridge's mastery breakdown shows exactly where you stand.
